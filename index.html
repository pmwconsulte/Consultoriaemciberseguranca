About Me

I am a Senior Cybersecurity Engineer with practical experience across telecommunications, enterprise security operations, cyber risk management, business continuity, security monitoring and information security governance.

My expertise includes Microsoft Sentinel, IBM QRadar, Splunk, Wazuh, cloud security, incident response, ISO 27001 implementation, vulnerability management, security compliance and cyber resilience.

My objective is to help organizations protect critical assets, reduce cyber risks and establish mature cybersecurity programs aligned with international best practices.
